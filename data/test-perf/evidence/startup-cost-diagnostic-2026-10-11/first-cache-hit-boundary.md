## Source facts

- `src/loader/lua_file.rs:139-153`: `compile_lua_file` starts `compile_start` immediately before `env.with_state(...)`, then adds the full elapsed duration of `load_cached_or_compile_for_chunk(...)` to both `lua_compile_time` and `lua_exec_time`. This timer is not limited to compiling source.
- `src/loader/lua_file.rs:297-334`: `load_cached_or_compile` hashes the source/chunk (`303`), checks `is_disabled` (`305`), looks up cached bytecode (`306-308`), and on a successful replay increments `cache_hits` and returns (`309-313`). `compile_with_rilua` runs inside the cache callback (`306-308`). Thus even an exact hit includes source/chunk hash, cache lookup and callback bytecode compilation in `compile_time`; it does not include `execute_lua_file` (`171-181`), which has its separate `call_time` timer.
- `src/loader/bytecode_cache.rs:190-203`: on a permitted read, `with_cached_bytecode` obtains the mutex, derives `pack_path`, calls `ensure_loaded`, then looks up the requested hash and runs the callback.
- `src/loader/bytecode_cache.rs:225-233`, `267-287`, `306-340`: on the first cache access in the process, `ensure_loaded` may open/read the pack (bounded by `MAX_PACK_SIZE`), validate and parse its header/entries into the in-memory bytes/index, before the requested key lookup/callback. This initialization is inside the `compile_lua_file` timer when that call is first. Later accesses skip pack initialization when `state.initialized` is true.
- `src/loader/bytecode_cache.rs:133-151`: first `cache_state()` use initializes its `OnceLock<Mutex<CacheState>>`; first `is_disabled()` use initializes a separate `OnceLock<bool>` and reads the cache enable/disable environment variables. These one-time initialization checks also occur within the outer compile timer when first invoked, although source does not establish their actual duration.
- `src/loader/lua_file.rs:115-135` snapshots and differences cumulative compile/call/cache counters per Lua file. Those counters attribute time spent in `compile_lua_file` to the file but do not split hash, initialization, pack I/O/parse, or bytecode replay compilation.
